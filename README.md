# Portable-Power-Station
I wanted to learn how to make a portable power station. Currently, I'm planning on building a 4kWh portable power station. I'm also planning on adding solar power eventually.

## Items used:
- EVE LiFePO4 cell 3.2V 330Ah x 4 (1056Wh/cell = 4224Wh total capacity when in 4 cells in series, 4S)
- 600W Inverter 12V to 110V 
- Bisida 4S BMS 12.8V 100A Lifepo4 Battery Management System
- DC 0-200V 0-300A Battery Monitor Meter with Shunt
- misc parts (fuses, cables, bus bars, etc)
- Anderson Powerpole connector and panel

## Future things to add:
- solar charger controller (MPPT)
- Portable solar panel
- Air vents
